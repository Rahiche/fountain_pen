# fountain_pen

Text written out by a fountain pen, stroke by stroke, the way a hand writes it. In Latin, Japanese and Arabic, for
Flutter.

## Why fountain_pen

- **Written, not faded in.** Each letter is drawn stroke by stroke, in the order a hand would draw it.
- **Japanese and Arabic too.** It writes 6,355 kanji in their stroke order. Arabic, Persian and Urdu are written right
  to left, with their letters joined.
- **It moves like a hand.** The pen slows into tight turns and speeds along the straights, by the two-thirds power law
  of human movement.
- **Real ink.** Heavy downstrokes, hairline upstrokes, and wet ink that pools where the pen lands. Every seed writes in
  a different hand.
- **Cameras.** Film the pen close up, or in 3D from the sky, low along the line, or circling it.
- **Your own font.** Trace any font you're licensed to use into a hand.
